Success Stories

From laundry service to digital platform.

WashApp is a mobile application designed to simplify everyday life by offering a complete on-demand laundry and delivery service.

The platform allows users to schedule pickup, washing, drying, and delivery of clothes within 24 hours.

Client challenge

A service ready to scale needed a stronger product foundation.

  • No defined product structure or UX/UI
  • Complex logistics operations
  • Multiple user types: customers and providers
  • Need for a scalable digital platform
Our solution

We turned the operation into a mobile-first product.

Eureka Dreams acted as a strategic technology partner, delivering product strategy, UX/UI design, prototyping, and custom software development.

  • Product strategy and roadmap
  • UX/UI design and interactive prototypes
  • Mobile app development
  • Custom software for operations
  • Scalable architecture
Technology & Strategy

The platform needed more than code — it needed product structure.

The work combined product decisions, user-centered design, agile execution, cloud infrastructure, and logistics automation to support a real service operation.
